## What is SFL Guro?

SFL Guro is a free helper app for Sunflower Land players. You open it in your browser, type in your Farm ID, press Sync, and it reads your farm and works out the numbers for you. No login, no wallet connection, nothing to install, and it works fine on a phone.

It is made by a player, for players. The goal is simple: spend less time guessing and more time playing.

## What it does

SFL Guro is a set of tools and calculators in one place. It looks at your own farm, your boosts and your skills, and shows you what each thing is worth for you. It covers farming, animals, fishing, cooking, honey, digging, crafting and the marketplace. It also has planning tools, guides for new players, and your farm profile with what your inventory is worth.

Everything uses live prices from the game, so the numbers stay close to what you will really get.

## How to use it

1. Go to [sfl-guro.github.io](https://sfl-guro.github.io/).
2. Type your Farm ID and press Sync.
3. Open any page from the menu. Everything will use your farm.
